Ticket 10 · vertical slice

Landing page

ready-for-agent

A visitor arriving logged out can tell in ten seconds whether this is the thing they want: a real pet record on the page, and the plain statement that the app never notifies.

Blocked by

05  Due dates: the ledger, the overdue stamp, mark done

What to build

Last, because it advertises what by then exists. Every existing answer to this problem either does far too much or will not stop nagging — pet apps bolt on notifications, insurance offers, activity tracking and reminders that arrive at 7am. A handler who wants a written record does not want an app with a red dot on it, and the landing page’s job is to say so and to show the record.

It carries the routes into signup and sign in, and nothing else.

Acceptance criteria

  • The landing page shows a real pet record, not an abstract illustration
  • It states that the app sends no email and no notification
  • It routes to signup and to sign in
  • A signed-in handler arriving at the root goes to their home screen instead
  • It has a desktop and a mobile layout

Done means

Every criterion above passes at the two seams the PRD fixed: the HTTP API against a real Postgres with RLS, the views and the constraints all live, and the rendered screen with HTTP stubbed at the network boundary. No test mocks the database, no test mocks a hook, and no test asserts on component internals — a test that breaks when nothing observable changed is a bug in the test. The desktop and mobile layouts are built together in this slice, not as a pass at the end: the slice is not done until both exist.
