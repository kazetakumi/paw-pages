Dependency chain

01 walking skeleton ├─ 02 auth screens └─ 03 a pet exists └─ 04 an entry exists ├─ 05 due dates │ ├─ 08 archiving (also needs 06) │ └─ 10 landing page └─ 06 the public page ├─ 07 pet photos (also needs 03) │ └─ 09 account └─ 08 archiving

Only 01 is unblocked at the start. 02 and 03 can run in parallel once it lands, and so can 05 and 06 once 04 does. 08 waits on both of those; 09 waits on photos because deleting an account has to remove them first.

Keeping this current

Status lives in two places — the badge on the ticket page and the row in the table above. Move both when a ticket moves. The four values are ready-for-agent, in-progress, done and blocked. The counts and the progress bar at the top of this page are written in, so they need updating too.

Out of scope for all ten tickets, restated here so it is not reopened: email or push reminders, vaccine schedule intelligence, weight tracking and charts, attachments, photo galleries, co-handlers and household sharing, search or filtering across entries, a printable PDF, native or offline builds, cost tracking, a microchip field, a vet on the pet, a fixed list of entry types, per-entry public visibility, and any scheduled job.
