02

It won't chase you

No emails. No notifications. No red dot at 7am. Due dates sit at the top of your home screen and overdue ones are stamped. That's the whole system.

14 JUL 2026 Biscuit — Rabies booster Overdue by 46 days
05 SEP 2026 Momo — Deworming In 7 days

  • Every pet's dates on one screen, oldest problem first.
  • Archived pets stop counting. Nothing nags you about a dog you've buried.
