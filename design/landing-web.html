01

Not just the shots

A page holds anything worth remembering about a pet. Type whatever the thing was — the app doesn't make you pick from a list.

02 JUN 2026 Vet visitLimping on the back right leg. Nothing found.

  • A date for what happened, and optionally a date for the next one.
  • A note in your own words — batch numbers, what the vet said, how she took it.
  • A photo and the basics — breed, colour, sex, when they were born.
  • Pets who've passed on stay, archived, with their history intact.
02

It won't chase you

No emails. No notifications. No red dot on your phone at 7am. Due dates sit at the top of your home screen, and overdue ones are stamped. That's the whole system — and it means Paw Pages never needs your phone number.

14 JUL 2026 Biscuit — Rabies booster Overdue by 46 days
05 SEP 2026 Momo — Deworming In 7 days
21 SEP 2026 Pepper — DHPP booster In 23 days
  • Every pet's due dates, on one screen, oldest problem first.
  • Overdue is impossible to miss and stays until you deal with it.
  • Archived pets stop counting. Nothing nags you about a dog you've buried.
03
